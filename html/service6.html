🧠 Pet Behavior & Training

A well-behaved pet is a happy companion. Our behavior and training services focus on positive reinforcement techniques to improve habits, reduce stress, and strengthen the bond between you and your pet.

🐶 Basic Obedience Training

Teaching essential commands and everyday discipline using gentle, reward-based methods.

  • Sit, stay, come training
  • Leash walking habits
  • Improved listening skills
  • Owner-pet communication

😌 Behavior Problem Correction

Address unwanted behaviors through structured and personalized training plans.

  • Excessive barking control
  • Aggression management
  • Chewing & destructive habits
  • Fear & anxiety behavior

🐾 Puppy & Kitten Training

Early training to build good habits and ensure proper development from a young age.

  • Toilet training guidance
  • Basic command introduction
  • Social behavior development
  • Routine & habit building

🧘 Socialization & Anxiety Care

Helping pets feel safe and confident in different environments and situations.

  • Interaction with people & pets
  • Separation anxiety support
  • Stress reduction techniques
  • Confidence building activities
